# Quantum Speedup | Master's Research Project

University of Warwick — Master of Mathematics (MMath)

## Overview

This project investigates the mathematical foundations of quantum algorithmic speedup, exploring how quantum algorithms can outperform classical algorithms under comparable computational models.

The research focuses on two mechanisms of quantum computation:

- **Grover's Algorithm**: How amplitude amplification provides a quadratic improvement in unstructured search.

- **Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE)**: How quantum algorithms exploit spectral information through phase estimation and the quantum Fourier transform.

Alongside the theoretical analysis, I developed original Python simulations to model and visualise the behaviour of Grover's algorithm.
